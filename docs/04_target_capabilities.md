# Target Capabilities — intervention options, not mandatory technologies

A credible modernization may include: canonical asset/configuration identity; temporal/provenance-aware telemetry; process dependency model; contextual risk; safety-aware response policy; recovery dependency model; evidence graph/knowledge model where justified; predictive degradation; read-only digital twin/simulation; bounded decision agents; human authority; TEVV; observability; and measurable operational outcomes.

Do not assume KG, digital twin, RAG or agents are automatically correct. Use them only when evidence shows they solve a real problem.
