# Domain Context

The fictional company operates 18 industrial sites across five regions. Plants use mixed-generation OT: PLC/DCS, SCADA/HMI, historians, safety systems, engineering workstations, gateways, CMMS/EAM, SOC/SIEM and vendor access.

The business wants a global risk and resilience command center, but current systems disagree about what assets exist, what state they are in, how cyber findings map to physical consequences, which safety barriers are active, and whether recovery plans are actually executable.

The goal is **not** to automate control. The goal is to establish trustworthy cyber-physical situational awareness, contextual risk, resilience decisions and governed action recommendations.
