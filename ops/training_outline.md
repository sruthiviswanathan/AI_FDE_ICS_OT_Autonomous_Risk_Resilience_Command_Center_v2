# Training outline

Audience: SOC, Process Eng, Safety, VP Ops (roles). Duration: workshop half-day.

1. Five states: observed / registered / operational interpretation / safety / decision authority.
2. Highest CVSS is not highest operational risk (EVAL-002).
3. CURRENT backup is not RecoveryReady (EVAL-005).
4. IsolationRecommendation ≠ IsolationExecution (EVAL-003/020).
5. UNKNOWN permission is not permission.
6. AI-disabled tables (EVAL-016).
7. Prompt injection stays content (EVAL-014/027).
8. How to read `GET /graph/slice` (hop ≤ 8, no estate dump).
