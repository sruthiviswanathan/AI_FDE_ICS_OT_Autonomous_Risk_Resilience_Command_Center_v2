# REL-03 — Elevator pitch (60–90 seconds)

**Spoken. ~170–220 words. FDE in the plant, not a pitch deck.**  
Do not claim ISO, plant dollars, or named Authorizers.

---

## Script (~75 seconds)

Leadership asked for an autonomous risk and resilience command center across eighteen plants. What we actually have is an estate that disagrees with itself: two hundred inventory conflicts, thousands of bad telemetry points, bypassed barriers, unapproved vendor sessions — and three pieces of legacy code that make it worse. Rank by CVSS. Isolate on HIGH. Call recovery ready because a backup flag says CURRENT.

That is the break. Not “we need more AI.”

The FDE ask is a **trusted advisory** path: keep five truths separate — observed, registered, operational, safety, and who is allowed to decide. Draft a packet with evidence. Do not execute.

Repo 3.0 plus this workbench do that on synthetic data. Contextual risk can outrank a 9.8 that cannot reach the unit. On CASCADE-001, SOC wants isolate at 08:47; process warns at 08:50; at 08:55 the product drafts **do not isolate**, execute false. Recovery is not ready just because backup is CURRENT. Thirty-one golden evals pass. AI is **off** by default; if it dies, the tables stay.

We will not invent MTT dollars in this room. We will not canary a controller.

**Ninety-day ask:** scale the advisory shadow. Name the humans in your process who may authorize. Measure clocks in tabletop. Moonshot only if those gates still hold. Do not scale isolation execute.

---

## 60-second cut (if standing only)

Eighteen plants, no single cyber-physical truth. Legacy ranks CVSS, isolates on HIGH, and trusts a CURRENT backup. We built an advisory command center that keeps disagreements visible and refuses unsafe isolate — including CASCADE at 08:55, execute false. AI off unless asked; evals green; no plant writes. Ask: scale shadow advice, not autonomy. Name your Authorizers outside this software. No live canary.
