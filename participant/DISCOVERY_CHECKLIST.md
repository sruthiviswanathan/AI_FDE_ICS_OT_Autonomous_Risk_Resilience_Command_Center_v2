# Discovery Checklist

- Reconcile CMDB/passive/CMMS asset identities.
- Quantify undocumented or unknown network paths.
- Find telemetry duplicates, unit mismatches, stale/uncertain data and temporal anomalies.
- Compare cyber severity with process criticality and compensating controls.
- Find safety barriers bypassed/degraded or overdue for proof test.
- Compare CMMS status with observed state.
- Identify unapproved remote access and weak MFA evidence.
- Trace three assets to process units and downstream business consequence.
- Quantify backup/runbook/restore-test weakness.
- Find cases where isolation could be operationally unsafe.
- Identify which decisions require human authority.
- Define eval gates before autonomy.
